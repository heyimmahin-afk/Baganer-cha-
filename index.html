01 The philosophy

Chaos is the raw material.
Ego is the finished shape.

Three hundred strikers are locked inside a facility built to destroy everything except the one thing that matters: the hunger to score. Every fragment of ability, every shattered assumption, every rejected version of yourself — scattered across the dark.

Scroll, and the pieces begin to fall into place. Not into a team. Into a single, complete ego — the one who decides the game.
